# Desktop monitoring and privacy

Idle detection, application tracking, website tracking, screenshots and productivity classification default to OFF. Retention defaults to 30 days with design bounds 1–90; offline leases default to zero, with a future maximum of 3600 seconds subject to privacy review. These limits are proposals, not active enforcement.

OWNER or ADMIN/MANAGER with monitoring.configure may configure. EMPLOYEE/CONTRACTOR cannot enable, disable or configure organizational collection. Stopping a work session or disconnecting a personal device must always remain possible and does not mutate organizational policy.

Before collection: publish clear employee notice, acknowledge exact policy version, obtain required OS permissions, display persistent visible collection status, and validate a signed device/user/organization-scoped lease. Changed policy requires renewed acknowledgment and authorization. Offline expiry stops collection, never extends cached authority. Device disconnect revokes authorization. No covert tracking, keylogging or password collection. Website capture must avoid secrets in URLs; screenshot redaction/exclusions and data minimization require security review. Productivity classifications require transparency and contestability.

Policy changes and report access are audited. Retention deletion is explicit, verifiable and scoped, including exports and device caches. Monitoring report permission is independent of policy management. Only policy and safety contracts exist: no collector, desktop agent, screenshot storage or OS access.
